05FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of software do you build?

We build custom software for any industry, including construction project management software for the building trades, Chrome and browser extensions, websites and web apps, fintech applications and games. If your business runs on a process that off-the-shelf tools don't handle well, we can build software around it.

How do you use AI, and how do you ensure quality?

We use modern AI development and production pipelines to build faster than was previously possible, but people stay in control. Experienced developers supervise every step, specialized AI agents perform deep-dive reviews of the code, and a developer reads and audits the code before it ships. Nothing is delivered unreviewed.

Do you work with clients outside Washington?

Yes. We're based in Auburn, WA and work with clients locally across the Puget Sound and Seattle area, and remotely with clients anywhere.

How do I get a quote?

Email czsoftwarellc@gmail.com with a short description of your project, your goals and any deadlines. We'll follow up to talk through the details and provide an estimate.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A focused tool or browser extension can move quickly, while larger platforms take longer. Contact us with your project details for a realistic timeline and estimate.
